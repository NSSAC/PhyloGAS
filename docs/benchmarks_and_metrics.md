## 1. Surveillance Sampling Recipes (`<Scenario>__<Algorithm>`)

In Stage 4 of the pipeline ([BeyondBaseline](https://github.com/NSSAC/BeyondBaseline)), ascertained clinical line-list cases (`02_simulated_linelists/linelist.csv.xz`) are subsampled under a fixed weekly sequencing budget (default `batch_size: 400` sequences/week). Each saved sample set in `03_sampled_datasets/` is identified by a **recipe** combining a **sampling scenario** and an **allocation algorithm**:

```text
<Stride>[-<Pool>_<Target>]__<Algorithm>
```

### 1.1 Allocation Algorithms

| Display Nomenclature | Recipe Slug (`__<Algorithm>`) | Subgroup Partition | Selection Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`SURS`** | `surs` | None (unstratified) | **Simple Uniform Random Sampling:** Draws a uniform random sample without replacement from all ascertained line-list cases in the pooling window. Ignores demographic/geographic targets (`1S__surs`, `4S__surs`). |
| **`LASSO50-Greedy`** | `lasso_greedy` | **50 LASSO macro-subgroups** | **LASSO Subgroup Discovery + Greedy Submodular Optimization:** Clusters the multi-way demographic $\times$ county cross-classification (`age`, `race`, `county`, `sex`) into $K = 50$ macro-subgroups via LASSO regression on historical incidence, then greedily selects samples each stride to minimize KL divergence against the target distribution over those 50 subgroups. |
| **`LASSO50-Stratified`** | `lasso_stratified` | **50 LASSO macro-subgroups** | **LASSO Subgroup Discovery + Proportional Stratified Sampling:** Uses the same $K = 50$ LASSO macro-subgroups as `LASSO50-Greedy`, but allocates quotas proportionally with random draws inside each subgroup rather than greedy submodular optimization. |
| **`LASSO1500-Greedy`** | `lasso1500_greedy` | **1,500 fine-grained LASSO subgroups** | High-granularity LASSO subgroup partition ($K = 1{,}500$) paired with greedy submodular selection. |
| **`Stratified`** | `stratified` | Full cross-classification (`age` $\times$ `race` $\times$ `county` $\times$ `sex`) | Classical proportional quota sampling directly over raw census/line-list strata without LASSO dimensionality reduction. |

### 1.2 Temporal Strides & Target Distributions (`<Scenario>`)

| Scenario Code | Temporal Stride (`S`) | Lookback Pool (`LL`) | Target Distribution | Operational Meaning |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`1S`** | 1 week | 1 week | Uniform over pool (`SURS` only) | Weekly uniform random baseline (`SURS · 1S`). |
| **`4S`** | 4 weeks | 4 weeks | Uniform over pool (`SURS` only) | 4-weekly pooled uniform random baseline (`SURS · 4S`). |
| **`1S-P`** | 1 week | — | **Census Population (`P`)** | Weekly allocation targeting state census demographic $\times$ county shares (`LASSO50-Greedy · 1S-P`). |
| **`4S-P`** | 4 weeks | — | **Census Population (`P`)** | 4-weekly allocation targeting state census demographic $\times$ county shares (`LASSO50-Greedy · 4S-P`). |
| **`1S-1_LL`** | 1 week | 1 week | **Line-List Incidence (`1_LL`)** | Weekly allocation targeting the observed 1-week clinical line-list distribution (`LASSO50-Greedy · 1S-1_LL`). |
| **`4S-4_LL`** | 4 weeks | 4 weeks | **Line-List Incidence (`4_LL`)** | 4-weekly allocation targeting the observed 4-week clinical line-list distribution (`LASSO50-Greedy · 4S-4_LL`). |
| **`1S-1_LL-P`** | 1 week | 1 week | **50/50 Blended (`1_LL-P`)** | Weekly allocation targeting a 50/50 blend of 1-week clinical line-list incidence and census population shares (`LASSO50-Greedy · 1S-1_LL-P`). |
| **`4S-4_LL-P`** | 4 weeks | 4 weeks | **50/50 Blended (`4_LL-P`)** | 4-weekly allocation targeting a 50/50 blend of 4-week clinical line-list incidence and census population shares (`LASSO50-Greedy · 4S-4_LL-P`). |

## 2. ABM Epidemiological & Waning Characterization Metrics

Computed from the raw EpiHiper simulation output (`output.csv.gz`) and empirical importation schedules (`data/seed_schedule/{state}-{variant}.csv`) by [`scripts/utils/characterize_exp3.py`](../scripts/utils/characterize_exp3.py) and `scripts/calc_re.py` (reported in [Table 1 of `docs/characterization/README.md`](characterization/README.md#1-epidemiological--waning-calibration-exp1-vs-exp2-vs-exp3)).

| Metric / Column | Symbol / Units | Direction | Definition & Computation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Total Wave Exposures** (`E1 Total`, `E2 Total`) | Count | Descriptive | Total number of synthetic agents transitioning into exposed state `E1` (Variant 1 / Alpha-like background wave) or `E2` (Variant 2 / Delta wave) across the 500-tick simulation (`tickZero = 2020-11-30`). |
| **Realized Generation Interval** (`E1 GI`, `E2 GI`) | $\text{GI}$ (days) | Descriptive | Mean elapsed time $t_{\text{exposed}}(\text{child}) - t_{\text{exposed}}(\text{parent})$ across all directed parent $\to$ child transmission pairs where `contact_pid >= 0`. Also computed within the 300-day painted window by [`src/phylogas/benchmark/sequence.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/sequence.py) (`gi_mean_days`, `gi_median_days`, `gi_q25_days`, `gi_q75_days`). |
| **Early-Cohort Reproductive Number** (`R0(1k)`, `R0(10k)`, `Re(50k)`) | Secondary cases / index case | Descriptive | Mean number of secondary transmissions caused by the first $1{,}000$, $10{,}000$, or $50{,}000$ index exposures of the wave (`E1` or `E2`), measuring effective transmissibility as local contact-network saturation progresses. |
| **Pre-Peak Importation Coverage** (`E2 Seeds <= Peak`) | Count (`%` of schedule) | Higher = less premature | Number (and percentage) of empirical `E2` importation events (`data/seed_schedule/{state}-2.csv`) that arrive on or before the simulated `E2` daily incidence peak tick. Diagnoses whether the simulated wave peaks prematurely before empirical seed lineages arrive (`1.6%` in `exp2` vs. `13.0%` in `exp3`). |
| **Window Reinfection Share** (`Window Reinf %`) | `%` of `E2` cases | Lower = less churn | Percentage of `E2` exposures inside the 300-day PhyloGAS painted window that occur in agents who have already experienced an earlier `E2` infection. |
| **Short-Gap Reinfection Rate** (`Gaps <= 30d / <= 60d`) | `%` of repeat infections | Lower = realistic waning | Among all agents with repeat exposures, the fraction whose inter-exposure time gap $\Delta t$ is $\le 30\text{ days}$ or $\le 60\text{ days}$. Diagnostic of exponential immunity waning (`mode = 0 d` in `exp1`, where rapid urban reinfection loops occur) vs. `Gamma(k=4, \theta=45.75\text{ d})` waning (`mode = 137.25 d` in `exp3`, where $\le 30\text{d}$ gaps drop to `0.03%`). |

## 3. Molecular-Clock Closure & Sequence Divergence Metrics

Computed by `phylogas benchmark sequence` ([`src/phylogas/benchmark/sequence.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/sequence.py)) into `05_benchmarks/sequence_divergence.csv` and `phylogas benchmark clock` ([`src/phylogas/benchmark/clock.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/clock.py) and [`src/phylogas/benchmark/lineage.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/lineage.py)) into `05_benchmarks/clock_estimates.csv`. See [`docs/clock_modes.md`](clock_modes.md) and [`docs/abm_contribution.md`](abm_contribution.md) for full derivations (reported in [Table 2 of `docs/characterization/README.md`](characterization/README.md#2-molecular-clock-closure--two-way-orthogonality)).

### The Fundamental Closure Identity

$$\mu_{\text{truth}} \;=\; \frac{\hat{\lambda}}{\text{GI}} \cdot \frac{365.25}{L} \cdot s_{\text{path}}$$

| Metric / Column | Symbol / Units | CSV Source (`column`) | Definition & Computation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Per-Transmission Substitution Count** | $\hat{\lambda}$ (subs / transmission) | `sequence_divergence.csv` (`mean_substitutions`) | Mean Hamming distance between parent and child genomes across all painted transmission edges in the 300-day window. Owned entirely by the Genetic Painter (`mutation_rate_per_cycle * L * E[cycles]`). |
| **Unchanged Transmission Fraction** | $P(\Delta = 0)$ | `sequence_divergence.csv` (`frac_unchanged`) | Fraction of painted parent $\to$ child transmission events with zero nucleotide substitutions ($\approx 0.711$ at $\hat{\lambda} = 0.343$). |
| **Naïve Implied Clock Rate** | $\hat{\lambda} / \text{GI}$ (`/yr` or `/site/yr`) | `sequence_divergence.csv` (`implied_subs_per_genome_per_year`) | Expected evolutionary rate along a single unbroken lineage before accounting for multi-seed coexistence or selection: $(\hat{\lambda} / \text{GI}) \times 365.25$ (divided by comparable genome length $L = 29{,}750$ for `/site/yr`). |
| **Pooled Ground-Truth Clock** | $\mu_{\text{truth}}$ (subs / site / yr) | `clock_estimates.csv` (`quantity == "mu_truth"`) | Ordinary least-squares (OLS) slope of root-to-tip Hamming distance (relative to Wuhan-Hu-1) on sampling date across all sequences in the arm, with no phylogenetic tree inference. |
| **Within-Chain Lineage Clock** | $\mu_{\text{lineage}}$ (subs / site / yr) | `clock_estimates.csv` (`quantity == "mu_lineage"`) | Fixed-effects within-chain OLS slope (allowing each imported transmission chain $c$ its own intercept $\alpha_c$). Measures the painter's pure evolutionary rate inside imported chains. |
| **Between-Chain Importation Slope** | $\mu_{\text{between\_chains}}$ (subs / site / yr) | `clock_estimates.csv` (`quantity == "mu_between_chains"`) | Tip-weighted OLS slope of chain mean divergence on chain mean sampling date. Captures the contribution of the empirical seed schedule's divergence trend. Satisfies $\mu_{\text{truth}} = w\,\mu_{\text{lineage}} + (1-w)\,\mu_{\text{between\_chains}}$, where $w$ (`within_date_variance_share`) is the within-chain share of sampling-date variance. |
| **Path-Length / Coexistence Factor** | $s_{\text{path}}$ (dimensionless) | Derived (`mu_truth / implied_per_site_yr`) | Ratio of the pooled root-to-tip slope $\mu_{\text{truth}}$ to the naïve single-lineage ratio $\hat{\lambda}/\text{GI}$. Reflects tip-weighted seed coexistence and transmission-chain survival variance ($\approx 0.844$ in `exp3`). |
| **TreeTime Inferred Clock** | $\mu_{\text{sim}}$ (subs / site / yr) | `clock_estimates.csv` (`quantity == "mu_sim"`) | Unconstrained root-to-tip molecular clock rate estimated by Nextstrain's `augur refine` (TreeTime) on the maximum-likelihood tree (`tree_raw.nwk`) built from the sampled subset. |
| **Root-to-Tip Temporal Signal** | $R^2 \in [0, 1]$ | `clock_estimates.csv` (`r_squared`) | Coefficient of determination ($R^2$) of the root-to-tip divergence regression on sampling date. Higher $R^2$ indicates stronger temporal clock signal ($0.314$ in `exp1` $\to$ $0.505$ in `exp3`). |

## 4. Phylogeographic Flow Reconstruction Metrics (`Mugration_Metrics.csv`)

Computed by `phylogas benchmark mugration` ([`src/phylogas/benchmark/mugration.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/mugration.py) and [`src/phylogas/benchmark/scoring.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/scoring.py)) into `05_benchmarks/Mugration_Metrics.csv` (reported in [Table 3 of `docs/characterization/README.md`](characterization/README.md#3-phylodynamic--surveillance-sampling-benchmarks-lasso50-greedy-vs-surs)).

### Matrix Construction & Alignment

1. **Ground-Truth Transition Matrix ($T_{ij}$):** Built from all directed parent $\to$ child transmission edges in the ABM ground truth where `county(parent) != county(child)`.
2. **Inferred Mugration Matrix ($W_{ij}$):** Built via upward Fitch-style parsimony ancestral state reconstruction on the transmission subgraph traced by the sampled tips ([`simulate_inference_and_matrix`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/mugration.py#L53-L148)), normalized using Nextstrain's symmetrized transition rate formulation:
   $$W_{ij} \;=\; \frac{1}{2\mu_{\text{geo}}} \left( \frac{q_{ij}}{\pi_j} + \frac{q_{ji}}{\pi_i} \right)$$
   where $q_{ij}$ is the inferred transition count from county $i$ to county $j$, $\pi_j$ is the equilibrium share of inferred nodes in county $j$, and $\mu_{\text{geo}}$ is the overall geographic jump rate.
3. **Off-Diagonal Vectorization:** Both $T$ and $W$ are aligned to the master alphabet of all $N$ counties in the state, normalized to sum to $1$, and flattened into off-diagonal vectors $\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{R}^{N(N-1)}$ ([`score_matrices`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/scoring.py#L86-L95)).

| Metric / Column | Symbol / Range | Direction | Definition & Operational Interpretation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Flow Proportions Cosine Similarity** (`cosine_similarity`) | $\text{Cos}(\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{w}) \in [0, 1]$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | $\frac{\mathbf{t} \cdot \mathbf{w}}{\|\mathbf{t}\|_2 \|\mathbf{w}\|_2}$. Measures how accurately the inferred county-to-county transition matrix recovers the **relative intensity proportions** of inter-county viral flow, independent of global scale. |
| **Flow Topology $F_1$ Score** (`topological_f1`) | $F_1 \in [0, 1]$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | Harmonic mean of precision and recall on the binarized support ($\mathbb{I}(t_{ij} > 0)$ vs. $\mathbb{I}(w_{ij} > 0)$) of non-zero inter-county edges ([`score_offdiagonals`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/scoring.py#L69-L74)). Measures whether surveillance **discovers the true network of county-to-county transmission bridges**, regardless of edge weight. |
| **Flow Intensity Correlation** (`pearson_r`) | $r \in [-1, 1]$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | Linear Pearson correlation coefficient between $\mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{w}$. Sensitive to high-volume urban-to-urban commuter corridors. |
| **Masked Mean Absolute Error** (`masked_mae`) | $\text{MAE}_{\text{active}} \ge 0$ | **Lower is better ($\downarrow$)** | Mean absolute difference $\frac{1}{|A|} \sum_{(i,j) \in A} |t_{ij} - w_{ij}|$ restricted to active edges $A = \{(i,j) : t_{ij} > 0 \text{ or } w_{ij} > 0\}$, preventing shared zero edges from artificially deflating the error. |
| **Cross-Regime Drift** (`mean_abs_delta_cos_exp1_exp3`) | $\|\Delta\text{Cos}\| \ge 0$ | **Lower is better ($\downarrow$)** | Mean per-state absolute shift $\frac{1}{5}\sum_{s} |\text{Cos}_{s,\text{exp3}} - \text{Cos}_{s,\text{exp1}}|$ when disease dynamics change from exponential waning (`exp1`) to Gamma waning (`exp3`). Quantifies **regime resilience** (insensitivity of a sampling strategy to shifts in epidemic reinfection dynamics). |
| **Cross-Regime Cosine Floor** (`min_cos_floor_10runs`) | $\min \text{Cos} \in [0, 1]$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | Worst-case single-state Cosine similarity across all 10 post-QC state-regime runs (`5 states` $\times$ `2 experiments`). Measures worst-case robustness across heterogeneous geographies and disease regimes. |

## 5. Ground-Truth Surveillance & Phylogenetic Tree Coverage Metrics (`AUC_truth_rankings.csv`)

Computed by `phylogas benchmark truth` ([`src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_runner.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_runner.py) and [`src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_metrics.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_metrics.py)) into `05_benchmarks/AUC_truth_rankings.csv` (reported in [Table 4 of `docs/characterization/README.md`](characterization/README.md#table-4-exp3-epidemic-truth--demographic-equity-coverage-lasso50-greedy-vs-surs)).

### Time-Normalized Trapezoidal AUC (`auc`)

Each surveillance metric is evaluated at every stride boundary $w \in \{\text{stride}, 2\cdot\text{stride}, \dots, W\}$ over the active sampling calendar (where week $0$ is the first week of the all-events window and $W$ is the last week with $\ge \text{min\_pool}$ reported cases). The time series $\{y(w)\}$ is summarized into a single scalar via **span-normalized trapezoidal integration** ([`_series_auc`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_runner.py#L120-L129)):

$$\text{AUC}(y) \;=\; \frac{1}{w_{\max} - w_{\min}} \int_{w_{\min}}^{w_{\max}} y(w)\,dw$$

so that 1-week (`1S`) and 4-week (`4S`) recipes are expressed on the exact same per-week unit scale.

### 5.1 Mean Reciprocal Distance (MRD) & Component Coverage Family ($\uparrow$ Higher is Better)

In [`src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_runner.py`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_runner.py#L16-L20), **Mean Reciprocal Distance (MRD)** measures how closely the sequenced sample set $S$ covers the true, hidden ABM transmission tree $G = (V, E)$ (built from all infections in `linelist_allevents.csv.xz`, including unascertained infections):

$$\text{MRD}(P_t, S) \;=\; \frac{1}{|P_t|} \sum_{u \in P_t} \frac{1}{d_G(u, S) + 1}$$

where $d_G(u, S) = \min_{s \in S} d_G(u, s)$ is the undirected shortest-path (geodesic) distance in transmission hops along the true ABM transmission graph from infection $u \in P_t$ to the nearest sequenced case $s \in S$ ($d_G(u, S) = \infty \implies \frac{1}{d_G+1} = 0$ if no sample lies in $u$'s transmission component).
- **Range & Direction:** $\text{MRD} \in [0, 1]$, where **$1.0$ means every infection in $P_t$ is directly sequenced ($d_G = 0$)** and values closer to $0$ mean infections sit many transmission hops away from any sequenced genome. **Higher MRD is always better ($\uparrow$)** ([`_HIGHER_IS_BETTER_PREFIXES`](../src/phylogas/benchmark/truth_runner.py#L92-L97)).

| `eval_type` in `AUC_truth_rankings.csv` | Target Set $P_t$ & Sample Set $S$ | Direction | Operational Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **`stride_component_coverage`** | Distinct transmission components (`component_id`) in stride window $[w - \text{stride} + 1, w]$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | **Clusters Reached:** Fraction of active ground-truth imported transmission chains (components) during the stride window that have at least one sequenced genome ($\frac{|\{\text{comp}(s) : s \in S_{\text{stride}}\}|}{|\{\text{comp}(u) : u \in P_{\text{stride}}\}|}$). |
| **`coverage_size_0`** ($C_0$) | Cumulative infections $u$ up to week $w$ in components of size $> 0$ (all components) vs. cumulative samples $S_{\le w}$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | **Full-Wave Phylogenetic Tree Coverage (MRD):** Cumulative Mean Reciprocal Distance across *every* infection in the epidemic up to week $w$. |
| **`coverage_size_10` / `100` / `1000`** ($C_{10}, C_{100}, C_{1000}$) | Cumulative infections $u$ up to week $w$ in transmission trees with $> 10$, $> 100$, or $> 1{,}000$ total cases | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | **Size-Filtered Tree Coverage (MRD):** Cumulative Mean Reciprocal Distance restricted to medium or large outbreak clusters. |
| **`8_week_rolling_tree_coverage`** | Infections $u$ and samples $S$ both restricted to the sliding 8-week window $[w - 7, w]$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | **8-Week Rolling Tree Coverage (MRD):** Measures real-time phylogenetic proximity of *recent* sequences (last 8 weeks) to *recent* infections. |
| **`equity_Senior_65+`**, **`equity_Adult_18-49`**, **`equity_School_5-17`**, **`equity_Preschool_0-4`**, **`equity_Older_Adult_50-64`** | Cumulative infections $u$ belonging to the specified age group vs. cumulative samples $S_{\le w}$ | **Higher is better ($\uparrow$)** | **Demographic Equity Tree Coverage (MRD):** Mean Reciprocal Distance along the transmission tree specifically for infections in each demographic age group. Quantifies how well surveillance covers vulnerable or lower-contact cohorts (such as `Senior_65+`). |

### 5.2 Prevalence & Demographic Distribution Error Family ($\downarrow$ Lower is Better)

| `eval_type` in `AUC_truth_rankings.csv` | Formula / Distance | Direction | Operational Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **`stride_variant_prevalence_error`** | $\sum_{v \in \mathcal{V}} \left|\hat{p}_{\text{stride}}(v) - p^{\text{true}}_{\text{stride}}(v)\right|$ ($L_1$ total variation error) | **Lower is better ($\downarrow$)** | **Variant Prevalence $L_1$ Error:** Sum of absolute differences between sampled sub-lineage proportions $\hat{p}(v)$ and true all-events infection proportions $p^{\text{true}}(v)$ in each stride window (over benchmark variants assigned by `phylogas assign-variants`, excluding `background`). |
| **`cumulative_infections`** | $D_{\text{KL}}\!\left(\hat{p}_{\le w}(\text{group}) \,\|\, p^{\text{true}}_{\le w}(\text{group})\right)$ | **Lower is better ($\downarrow$)** | **Cumulative Infection Demographic KL Divergence:** Kullback–Leibler divergence from the true cumulative infection distribution across demographic $\times$ county groups (`age`, `race`, `county`, `sex`) to the cumulative sequenced sample distribution. |
| **`stride_window_infections`** | $D_{\text{KL}}\!\left(\hat{p}_{\text{stride}}(\text{group}) \,\|\, p^{\text{true}}_{\text{stride}}(\text{group})\right)$ | **Lower is better ($\downarrow$)** | **Stride-Window Infection Demographic KL Divergence:** Same KL divergence evaluated within each individual stride window $[w - \text{stride} + 1, w]$. |
